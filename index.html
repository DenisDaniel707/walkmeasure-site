How it works

Set the phone down. Feel the buzz. Move on.

1Clip the phone onto a selfie stickTurn on "Selfie stick" in the app. No more bending down at every point.

2Set it down and hold it stillFlat on the ground at the first corner, for about a second. The point records itself.

3Feel the buzz in the handleThe phone gives a strong buzz and a beep: your signal to move on.

4Carry it onEvery meter or more, and at every corner, set it down again.

5Finish where you startedThe outline closes and the app shows the area, the sides and the measured error.

FAQ

Good to know

Another question? Write to us at contact@walkmeasure.com.

Do I need to buy a device or accessory?

No. WalkMeasure measures with the motion sensors already built into your iPhone: no wheel, laser, sensor or clip-on to buy, pair or charge. A selfie stick helps you reach the corners without bending down, but it is optional.

Does it use the camera, GPS or internet?

No. Only the iPhone's accelerometer and gyroscope. Measuring works offline, indoors, under trees and anywhere GPS is weak. You only need the internet to buy Pro.

How accurate is it?

It depends on calibration and on how often you set the phone down. Every result comes with its margin of error, and returning to the start checks it. For critical dimensions, check with a tape measure.

Can I use the result for the land registry or a sale?

No. Official documents need a licensed surveyor. WalkMeasure quickly tells you what to expect, so you decide what is worth a surveyor's visit.

Do I need to calibrate the phone?

Once, at the start: rest it on a table on each of its six sides, about a minute in all. The app guides you.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics. You share projects yourself, as PDF, CSV or DXF, only when you want to.

Which phone do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How much does it cost?

The app is free for one project. WalkMeasure Pro is $3.99 a month, cancelled any time, or $49.99 once for good. Both through the App Store. See pricing.

Can I pay once instead of subscribing?

Yes, for a limited time. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase of $49.99: every Pro feature, for good, including future updates. It belongs to your Apple ID, so Restore Purchases brings it back on a new iPhone, and it stays yours after the offer ends.
